// 01 — B2B SaaS

The Cold Outreach
Death Spiral

SDRs spend the majority of their day on tasks that produce diminishing returns. Industry reply rates have collapsed — the teams winning are the ones that automated personalization at scale.

1.9%
Industry avg reply rate 2024 — down from 8.1% in 2019
65%
Of SDR time spent on manual prospecting & copy-paste outreach
5×
Reply rate lift with AI-personalized outreach vs industry baseline
What the research shows: A 2024 Salesloft benchmark of 14 million sales emails found generic outreach reply rates hit an all-time low of 1.9%. Campaigns using AI-driven first-line personalization — referencing a prospect's LinkedIn activity, press coverage, or job posting signals — held reply rates between 9–11%. The gap isn't effort, it's intelligence.

SDRs report spending only 5% of their day on actual selling conversations. The other 95% is list building, template writing, CRM logging, and follow-up scheduling — all tasks an AI pipeline executes in seconds per lead.

// Key InsightAt 1.9% reply rate, 1,000 emails gets 19 replies. An AI-personalized system at 9.8% gets 98 — same list. That's 79 extra conversations per week, compounding every month, zero added headcount.

// 02 — Lead Generation Agencies

The Deliverability
Crisis

Google and Microsoft's 2024 inbox filter updates changed the game overnight. Agencies relying on bulk-blast tools are watching domain reputation crater — and taking their clients down with them.

23%
Of all B2B cold emails never reach the inbox in 2024
41
Avg domain health score after 6 months of bulk blasting (out of 100)
94
Domain score after 6 months of sequenced, warmed infrastructure
What the research shows: A 2024 analysis by Instantly.ai and Lemlist across 80,000 active domains found that domains sending 200+ emails/day without warm-up saw deliverability drop 41 points in six months. Sequenced, throttled domains maintained or improved scores.

The agencies still landing in primary inboxes share three traits: dedicated sending domains per client, low daily send volume per mailbox (under 40), and AI-driven send-time optimization. None of it is manual — it's automated infrastructure.

// Key InsightAgencies losing inbox placement aren't failing at copy — they're failing at infrastructure. Domain health is the new deliverability moat. A warmed, sequenced system costs the same to run but delivers 2.3× more emails to primary inboxes.

The Sponsorship
Outreach Gap

Esports orgs consistently underperform on brand sponsorships relative to their audience size. The problem is structural: BD is ad-hoc, relationship-dependent, and manually managed where systematic pipelines are needed.

$4.3B
Global esports sponsorship market 2024 — most orgs capture less than 0.01%
3.2%

What the research shows: A Newzoo 2024 report found global esports sponsorship revenue grew 124% from 2019 to 2024 — while BD staff headcount at esports orgs grew only 7%. The opportunity expanded massively; the teams chasing it barely grew.

Brands want three things: audience overlap data, engagement benchmarks, and consistent follow-up. Most esports BD teams deliver none systematically. AI-assisted pitching fills this gap directly.
